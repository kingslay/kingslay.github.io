Tracy Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Tracy handles information when you use the Tracy media player and its optional Google Drive integration.

Tracy is a media player for Apple platforms, including iPhone, iPad, Apple TV, and Mac. Tracy is developed by kintan.

By using Tracy, you acknowledge the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

1. Information We Collect and Access

Tracy is designed to minimize the collection of personal information. The information accessed by Tracy depends on the features you choose to use.

1.1 Local Media

Tracy can access video, audio, subtitle, image, and other media files that you make available to the application through local storage or supported file providers.

Tracy uses this information only to provide media browsing, organization, playback, and related functionality.

1.2 Network Media

Tracy can access media from network sources that you explicitly configure or select.

Network addresses, media URLs, connection information, and related metadata may be processed as necessary to connect to those sources and play the requested media.

1.3 Google Account and Google Drive

Tracy provides an optional integration with Google Drive.

When you choose to connect your Google account, Tracy requests access to the Google APIs and Google Drive data necessary to provide the Google Drive functionality that you request.

Depending on the Google OAuth permissions you grant, Tracy may access the following information:

  • Google account information required to authenticate and authorize the connection.
  • Google Drive files and folders required to browse your Drive.
  • File names, file identifiers, MIME types, sizes, timestamps, folder relationships, and other file metadata required by the application.
  • The contents of media files that you explicitly select for playback.

Tracy does not request your Google Account password. Authentication is performed through Google's OAuth authorization system.

2. How We Use Information

Tracy uses information only for the purposes necessary to provide the features you request.

2.1 Media Playback

Local, network, and cloud media information may be used to:

  • Browse and display your media files.
  • Display media metadata.
  • Load and play video and audio.
  • Load subtitles and related media resources.
  • Provide playback controls and media information.
  • Remember local application settings and playback preferences.

2.2 Google Drive

Google user data obtained through Google APIs is used only to provide Google Drive functionality that is visible and available to you in Tracy.

Specifically, Google Drive data may be used to:

  • Authenticate your Google account.
  • Connect Tracy to your Google Drive.
  • Browse your Google Drive files and folders.
  • Display Google Drive file metadata.
  • Allow you to select media files from Google Drive.
  • Access selected media files for playback.

Tracy does not use Google user data for advertising, behavioral profiling, credit evaluation, lending decisions, or unrelated purposes.

3. Google User Data and Limited Use

Tracy's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs complies with the Google API Services User Data Policy , including its Limited Use requirements.

Google user data obtained through Google APIs is used only to provide or improve user-facing features that are available in Tracy and directly related to the functionality requested by the user.

Tracy does not sell Google user data.

Tracy does not transfer Google user data to advertising platforms, data brokers, information resellers, or other third parties for advertising, retargeting, personalized advertising, interest-based advertising, credit-worthiness assessment, or lending purposes.

Tracy does not use Google user data to train or improve generalized artificial intelligence or machine learning models.

Tracy does not use Google APIs to generate, create, distribute, or facilitate non-consensual intimate imagery, including AI-generated non-consensual intimate imagery.

4. Sharing of Information

Tracy does not sell, rent, or trade your personal information.

Tracy does not share Google user data with third parties for their independent use.

Information may be transmitted to Google when Tracy communicates with Google APIs. Such communication is necessary to provide the Google Drive functionality that you have requested and authorized.

Tracy may also communicate with network services that you explicitly configure or select when you use network media features.

Tracy may disclose information when required by applicable law, regulation, legal process, or a valid governmental request.

5. Storage and Security

5.1 Local Storage

Tracy may store application settings, playback information, authentication credentials, cached information, and other data locally on your Apple device when required to provide application functionality.

Google OAuth credentials and tokens used by Tracy are stored locally on your Apple device using the security mechanisms provided by the Apple operating system, where applicable.

5.2 Tracy Servers

Tracy does not operate a backend service that permanently stores copies of your Google Drive media files.

Tracy does not permanently upload your Google Drive files to a Tracy server for storage or processing.

When you play a media file from Google Drive, Tracy may download or buffer portions of the file directly to your device as necessary for playback.

5.3 Security

Tracy uses reasonable technical measures provided by the Apple platform and the applicable Google APIs to protect information processed by the application.

No method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure. You are responsible for protecting access to your Apple devices and Google account.

6. Data Retention and Deletion

6.1 Google Authentication Information

Tracy retains Google OAuth credentials and tokens locally on your Apple device only for as long as they are needed to maintain the authorized Google Drive connection and provide the functionality you request.

Tracy does not retain your Google Account password.

Tracy does not store Google OAuth credentials on a Tracy-operated server.

6.2 Google Drive Data

Tracy accesses Google Drive files and metadata only when necessary to provide the Google Drive features you request.

Tracy does not permanently retain copies of your Google Drive files on Tracy-operated servers.

Temporary data or cached media may be stored locally on your Apple device as necessary for media playback. Such temporary local data is subject to the storage and cache behavior of Tracy and the Apple operating system.

6.3 Disconnecting Google Drive

You can disconnect your Google account from Tracy at any time through the Google Drive account settings provided by Tracy.

After you disconnect your Google account, Tracy will no longer use the associated OAuth authorization to make new Google Drive requests.

You may also revoke Tracy's Google account access through your Google Account security settings.

6.4 Deleting Local Tracy Data

You can delete locally stored Tracy data by using the data-management controls provided by Tracy or by deleting Tracy from your Apple device, subject to the storage behavior of the Apple operating system.

Deleting Tracy from your device does not delete files stored in your Google Drive.

Google Drive files remain under your control and can be deleted directly through Google Drive.

6.5 Data Deletion Requests

If you have questions about the deletion of personal information or Google user data associated with Tracy, you may contact us at:

kingslay@icloud.com

We will process valid deletion requests in accordance with applicable law and our actual data retention practices.

7. Google Account Access and Revocation

You can review or revoke Tracy's access to your Google Account through your Google Account security settings.

When Google access is revoked, Tracy can no longer obtain new Google user data using the revoked authorization.

Revoking access to Tracy does not delete your Google Drive files. Your Google Drive files remain in your Google account unless you separately delete them.

8. Third-Party Services

Tracy may interact with third-party services when you explicitly choose to use functionality provided by those services.

8.1 Google

Tracy uses Google APIs to provide optional Google Drive integration.

Google's handling of information is governed by Google's own privacy policies and terms.

For more information, please visit:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • Google API Services User Data Policy

9. Children's Privacy

Tracy is not specifically directed toward children under the age of 13.

Tracy does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 through a Tracy-operated online service.

If you believe that a child has provided personal information to us, please contact us so that we can review the situation and take appropriate action.

10. International Users

Tracy is available on Apple platforms in multiple countries and regions.

Depending on the services and network sources you use, information may be processed in countries other than your country of residence.

By using Tracy and its optional online integrations, you acknowledge that such processing may occur as necessary to provide the requested functionality and comply with applicable law.

11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when Tracy's functionality, data practices, legal requirements, or Google API requirements change.

When this Privacy Policy is updated, the "Last updated" date at the top of this page will be changed.

We recommend reviewing this Privacy Policy periodically to stay informed about how Tracy handles information.
